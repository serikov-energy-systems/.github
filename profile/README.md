### Energy systems · Mikhail Serikov

Energy system and grid models for planning under weather, climate and policy uncertainty.
Self-directed research studies on public data by [Mikhail Serikov](https://github.com/michaelserikov) (M.Sc. Ocean and Climate Physics, Universität Hamburg). Each README states what is observed, what is assumed and how the results were checked.

#### Weather, climate and adequacy
- **[de-power-adequacy](https://github.com/serikov-energy-systems/de-power-adequacy)** (flagship): resource adequacy of the German power system across 35 weather years (LOLE, EENS, ELCC) and under CMIP6 climate change factors
- [eu-weather-years-pypsa](https://github.com/serikov-energy-systems/eu-weather-years-pypsa): which weather year to design a renewable power system for (PyPSA capacity expansion, 40 weather years)
- [eu-power-stress-storylines](https://github.com/serikov-energy-systems/eu-power-stress-storylines): stressful events in the European power system from 2015-2025 observations to SSP3-7.0 storylines (ERA5, CESM2, PyPSA)

#### Energy system models
- [sweden-biomass-times-mini](https://github.com/serikov-energy-systems/sweden-biomass-times-mini): TIMES-inspired energy system model of Sweden 2025-2050, forest biomass allocation (GAMS)
- [messageix-westeros-residual-emissions](https://github.com/serikov-energy-systems/messageix-westeros-residual-emissions): scale-up rates and carbon removal in the MESSAGEix Westeros teaching model
- [de-district-heating](https://github.com/serikov-energy-systems/de-district-heating): investment and dispatch of a district heating network on the way to 2045 (Pyomo, HiGHS)
- [nordic-hydro-unit-commitment](https://github.com/serikov-energy-systems/nordic-hydro-unit-commitment): unit commitment of the German thermal fleet and water values of Norwegian hydropower (SDP)

#### Grids and planning
- [de-bidding-zones](https://github.com/serikov-energy-systems/de-bidding-zones): one German bidding zone against a north/south split on the open transmission grid (flow-based, redispatch, nodal benchmark)
- [de-feeder-storage-risk](https://github.com/serikov-energy-systems/de-feeder-storage-risk): battery or reinforcement on a medium-voltage feeder, two-stage planning with EVPI by uncertainty source
- [tyndp-exit-tracing](https://github.com/serikov-energy-systems/tyndp-exit-tracing): what happens to German transmission investments after they leave the TYNDP

**Related:** storm damage to the grid in [serikov-climate-risk](https://github.com/serikov-climate-risk) and market valuation of storage in [serikov-power-markets](https://github.com/serikov-power-markets)
